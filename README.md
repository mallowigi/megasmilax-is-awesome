# Megasmilax is Awesome

Megasmilax is a comprehensive collection of themes for JetBrains IDEs, designed to provide a variety of color schemes and styles to enhance your coding experience. Whether you prefer dark, light, or high-contrast themes, Megasmilax has something for everyone.

## 🎨 Included Theme Collections

Megasmilax features several popular theme collections, totaling over 100 variations:

- **Bearded**: A massive collection including Anthracite, Arc, Monokai, Solarized, Milkshake, and more.
- **Andromeda**: Modern and sleek themes including Aurora, Solar, Forest, and Nebula.
- **Lucide**: A diverse set of themes like Obsidian, Midnight, Dracula, Tokyo Night, Nord, and many others.
- **Mythos**: Themes inspired by Greek mythology like Olympus, Hades, Poseidon, and Nyx.
- **Noctis**: Sophisticated dark and light themes including Sereno, Obscuro, Azureus, and Lux.
- **Panda**: The classic Panda Dark theme.

## 🚀 Installation

1. Open your JetBrains IDE (IntelliJ IDEA, WebStorm, PyCharm, etc.).
2. Go to **Settings/Preferences** > **Plugins**.
3. Search for **Megasmilax is Awesome** in the Marketplace.
4. Click **Install**.
5. After installation, go to **Settings/Preferences** > **Appearance & Behavior** > **Appearance**.
6. Select your preferred Megasmilax theme from the **Theme** dropdown.
